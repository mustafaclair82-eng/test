How to Play PSP Games

Loading your ROM

Obtain your legally backed-up PSP game file in .ISO or .CSO format. Drag and drop the file anywhere into the main drop area, or click "Select ROM File".

Controller & Gamepad Support

Connect any USB or Bluetooth gamepad (Xbox, PlayStation, 8BitDo) before or during gameplay. EmulatorJS auto-detects and maps standard gamepad layouts automatically.

Save States

Click "Save State" on the control bar at any point during gameplay to create a instant snapshot. Click "Load State" to restore your exact progress.
